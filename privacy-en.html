Privacy Policy

{{ site.app_name }} app · Last updated: 16 September 2026

In short: your birth details stay on your phone. There is no account, no copy on our servers, and no analytics or advertising tracking. The only thing that leaves your phone is place search: the text you type, and the coordinates of the place you pick, go to Apple Maps to find the place and its time zone.

Who is responsible

{{ site.provider_name }}, an individual, {{ site.provider_place_en }}. Contact: {{ site.contact_email }}

What we collect

Only what you type into the app yourself:

  • Your name — used only to address you. You can leave it empty.
  • Your birth date.
  • Your birth time — you can leave it empty if you don't know it, in which case your rising sign and houses are not calculated.
  • Your birth place — the name, coordinates and time zone of the place you picked.
  • The two focus areas you chose.
  • What you marked on suggestions — "done", "tried", "not today".

Nothing else. We do not ask for an email address, a phone number, your gender, your contacts, your photos or any health data. The app has no location permission and never asks where you are.

Where it lives

All of it stays on your phone: in the app's own storage, and in the shared storage on the same phone that the home screen widget reads. Your birth chart and your daily and weekly content are calculated on the phone.

We keep no copy. You do not create an account and you do not sign in. We use no analytics, no third-party crash reporting, no advertising identifier and no third-party SDKs. The app contains no third-party packages at all.

The one thing that leaves your phone: place search

When you search for your birth place, the text you type is sent to Apple Maps to find the place. When you pick one, its coordinates are sent to Apple to look up the time zone. We do not attach your name or birth date to those requests. These lookups are covered by Apple's privacy policy.

With no network, search is unavailable and the app offers to continue with the place name you typed and an approximate location derived from a time zone. In that case nothing leaves the phone.

Content files

The app can download newer versions of its text and number files. Those requests are plain file downloads: they carry no personal data and no identifier for you. This version has no server configured, so nothing is downloaded. The app never downloads or runs code; the program that does the calculations ships inside the app.

Subscription

Payment is handled by Apple. We never see, receive or store your card details. Apple only tells the app whether your subscription is active; that is verified on the phone and stays on the phone.

Notifications

If you turn notifications on, the phone schedules them itself from content it already calculated. We use no push notification service; no device token is created or sent anywhere. While both switches are off, permission is not even requested.

Deleting

Verilerimi sil ("Delete my data") in the Sen tab removes your birth details, your chart, the calculated days and weeks, the suggestions you marked, the lines written for the widget and any scheduled notification from the phone. It happens immediately and cannot be undone. The app returns to the state it was in when first installed.

There is nothing to request from us, because we hold nothing. There is no account to delete.

Your rights

Because the person responsible is in Türkiye, Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK, Law No. 6698) applies. You exercise your rights of access, correction and deletion directly in the app: every piece of information is visible and editable in the Sen tab, and deletion takes one tap. There is no access or deletion request to address to us, because we hold no personal data. If that ever changes, this document changes at the same time.

Children

The app is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update this page and the date above. If your data ever starts leaving the phone, we will say so here plainly.
